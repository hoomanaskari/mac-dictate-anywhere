# Dictate Anywhere 2.9.0

- Choose your own cancellation shortcut, or clear it and cancel from the menu bar.
- Optional hold-to-cancel is enabled by default: hold the shortcut for one second to cancel. A quick Esc tap lets you keep working in another app without losing your dictation.
- Cancelled sessions are now saved privately on your Mac for 24 hours by default.
- Continue a cancelled session from History to restore your words and resume recording. Stop to finish one combined dictation; cancelling again preserves both parts.
- Recover text saves a cancelled dictation to History without recording or pasting. Continued dictations copy the finished text when the original destination app is unavailable.
- Added Stop and Cancel actions to the menu bar, and replaced the Escape shortcut symbol with the clearer Esc label.
