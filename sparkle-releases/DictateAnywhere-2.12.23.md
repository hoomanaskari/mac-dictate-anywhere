## Dictate Anywhere 2.12.23

- AssemblyAI now streams to STT-3.7 in real time by default. One session covers your whole dictation, so long recordings are no longer split into 100-second chunks, and the live preview shows AssemblyAI's own transcript as you speak.
- Custom vocabulary is sent to the streaming model as keyterms. Polished mode still cleans up the full transcript after you stop.
- If the stream drops, the app sends the recording through the Dictation API instead. Turn streaming off in Speech Model > AssemblyAI to keep the previous behavior.
