## Dictate Anywhere 2.12.20

- Dictate for longer with AssemblyAI: recordings are transcribed in overlapping chunks while the on-device live preview stays available.
- Improved continuity between chunks, with automatic retranscription when a boundary cannot be joined confidently.
- Audio and completed chunks are saved locally so failed requests can be retried without starting over.
- Added **Copy saved text** and **Export audio…** to cancelled sessions in History. Saved text may be a partial preview; audio exports as WAV.
- Readable recordings interrupted before recovery metadata was saved now appear in History after relaunch.
- Long dictations in Polished mode receive one cleanup pass over the combined transcript, with the complete raw text retained as a fallback.
